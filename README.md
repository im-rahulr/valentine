

### 💖 Be My Valentine

**Be My Valentine** is a playful and minimalist web app that poses the classic question: “Will you be my Valentine?” Users are presented with two options—**Yes** or **No**—accompanied by a heartwarming design that adds a personal touch to the experience.([formyvalentine.netlify.app][2])

---

### 🌟 Features

* **Simple & Elegant Design**: A clean interface that focuses on the central question, ensuring an uncluttered user experience.
* **Interactive Choices**: Users can respond with a simple "Yes" or "No," making the interaction straightforward and fun.
* **Responsive Layout**: Optimized for various devices, ensuring a seamless experience on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.
* **Lightweight & Fast**: Built with minimal dependencies to ensure quick load times and smooth performance.

---

### 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **Deployment**: Hosted on Netlify for reliable and fast delivery
